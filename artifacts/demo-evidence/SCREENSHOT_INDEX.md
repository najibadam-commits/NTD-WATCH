Recommended presentation sequence: `01`, `02`, (`03`), `04`, `05`, `06`, `07-why-this-priority-focused` (or `07c`). The GIS captures `08l`, `08m` and `08n` may be used as images of a map with legible, non-overlapping labels and "n/s" in place of small counts, captioned as a display rule on synthetic data (not as proof of privacy protection). The original `08-gis-priority.png` is not to be shown.

**Disclosure re-check (see `FINAL_GIS_DISCLOSURE_REVIEW.md`).** After these captures, four disclosure defects were fixed at the shared aggregation boundary (overlapping sums, the completion percentage, the signal band, and lone hidden weekly or risk-level cells). On the synthetic seed the values shown in `08l`, `08m`, `08n` and `08o` did not change (the same cells show "n/s"), so the classifications above stand. They remain images of a client-side display rule over a mock API, and cross-request differencing is not defended.

**Limits that apply to every GIS image.** The prototype applies a small-count display rule to selected published programme views and exports. This is a client-side display measure, not a security control. Unresolved: differencing across separate queries and date ranges; exact WASH percentages and the vulnerability index; staff counts; direction labels and signal bands that can narrow hidden counts; the facility referral inbox, which returns record-level data; full records held in the browser database and mock server; the threshold of 5 is a placeholder not justified by specialist review; and there has been no independent privacy review or production-security validation.

**Not for use:** `07b-why-this-priority-full-page-context.png` (diagnostic and historical only; it is not a fallback for any shot), `08o`, and the original `08-gis-priority.png`.

**Priority CSV.** Downloaded as `DEMO-SYNTHETIC-ntd-watch-priority-communities.csv` (the evidence screenshots and logs predate this filename change); its first row also identifies it as demonstration data. It must not be distributed without the synthetic-data context and an explanation of the limits above.
