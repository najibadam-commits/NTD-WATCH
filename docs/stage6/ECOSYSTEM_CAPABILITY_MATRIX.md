# Ecosystem capability matrix

**Status:** evidence-grounded comparison, created 2026-10-18 (Stage 6 evidence-integration v2). It is **external evidence plus unknowns**. It contains no stakeholder evidence, does not show that NTD-WATCH is unique, needed or better, and does not choose an outcome.

**How to read it.** Every cell separates three levels that must never be merged:

- **[G] Global capability:** what a platform or source says it can do.
- **[N] Nigerian implementation or configuration:** what is set up in a Nigerian programme. Unknown unless a source says so.
- **[F] Actual field practice:** what workers and programmes do. **Unknown for every row** until stakeholders confirm it.

"Overlap" says where NTD-WATCH's proposed function would duplicate something that exists, as a **hypothesis to check**, not a conclusion. Evidence IDs refer to `STAGE6_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` section 6 (all external, none stakeholder-validated). Statements marked "per brief" were relayed by the sponsor's briefs and not independently verified by me.

**Rows 1 to 5 are the minimum rows the brief requires. Rows 6 to 14 were added by the project team and are not from the brief** (the brief's row list ended after row 5; the sponsor should confirm whether more were intended).

## Reading across the rows

1. **Rows 1 to 5, 9 and 10 show that signal capture, CHW capture, offline capture, NTD data, reporting and WASH-NTD planning each have a documented counterpart,** global or Nigerian. None of these is a defensible difference for NTD-WATCH.
2. **Rows 7, 8 and 11 are the decisive unknowns:** whether referral, referral status, follow-up closure and community-based surveillance triangulation are already supported on DHIS2 in the target Nigerian settings, and whether they are used. If they are, the likely evidence direction is INTEGRATE; if they exist but are unused, the question becomes why; if absent, the gap hypothesis (H1b) survives to the next test. No outcome is chosen here.
3. **Row 6 is bounded by safety:** recognition, diagnosis and AI image classification are not NTD-WATCH's proposition.
4. **Every [N] and [F] cell marked unknown needs Nigerian stakeholder confirmation** (HIS, NTD programme, CHW, supervisor, WASH) before any difference is claimed.
5. The matrix does not say whether the post-signal gap is **measurable**. Candidate measures to be collected, not assumed, are listed in `STAGE6_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` section 4.2.
