# Stage 6 evidence register

**Status:** master register, updated 2026-10-18 for the evidence-integration update. It holds **62 original rows** (3 project facts verified from the repository, 1 in progress, 58 open) plus **15 external-evidence items XE-01 to XE-15** (9 evidence found, 6 in progress; several of those are partial or per-brief only). No stakeholder, expert, legal, ethics, prototype-session or management evidence has been collected. **No item is VALIDATED.** External evidence is never marked stakeholder-validated.

## 1. Use

- Source types: project fact, external research, stakeholder evidence, expert evidence, prototype session, legal or ethics evidence. The XE section (6) uses the ten-field schema; WS rows use the table.
- One row per piece of evidence needed. Add rows as new questions arise; never delete a row (mark DEFERRED or note why it was withdrawn).
- Each finding records its **class**: PF project fact, EF external finding, SS stakeholder statement, OB observation, AS assumption, UK unknown (see `STAGE6_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` section 7). Write the class at the start of the Finding cell.
- Cite source, role (not name unless agreed), date and method. A single source is Low confidence until corroborated.
- A finding of "no evidence" or "absence" is recorded as UK, never as "does not exist".
- Disconfirming evidence is recorded with the same care as confirming evidence.
- Interview notes, recordings and completed forms are stored under the programme's governance, not in this repository; the register holds only the coded finding and a reference.

## 2. Status definitions

| Status         | Meaning                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| OPEN           | Not started                                                                                                        |
| IN PROGRESS    | Collection under way                                                                                               |
| EVIDENCE FOUND | Evidence obtained and recorded, not yet corroborated or reviewed by the validation lead                            |
| VALIDATED      | Corroborated by at least two independent sources (or confirmed by the owner of the system or process) and reviewed |
| BLOCKED        | Cannot proceed; reason and unblocker in Next action                                                                |
| DEFERRED       | Intentionally postponed with reason and review date                                                                |

Project facts about this repository are marked EVIDENCE FOUND when verified by reading the repository or running it; they are not stakeholder-validated.

## 3. Confidence

High: primary source or direct observation, corroborated. Medium: primary source, uncorroborated, or secondary source, corroborated. Low: single secondary source, memory, or an assumption. "—" until a finding exists.
