# Differentiation validation (Nigeria-first, ecosystem-aware)

**Status:** hypotheses and test design, updated 2026-10-18 with external evidence from the Nigeria-first landscape research. **Nothing here establishes that NTD-WATCH is unique, effective, clinically accurate, cost-effective, compliant or ready for deployment.** Uniqueness is not claimed and is not the aim. The correct result of this work may be that the value already exists elsewhere, or can be delivered inside an existing platform.

Every statement below is labelled with one of: **project fact (PF)**, **external evidence (EF)**, **stakeholder evidence (SS)**, **expert evidence (XS)**, **assumption (AS)**, **unknown (UK)**. External evidence entries are in `STAGE6_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` section 6 (IDs `XE-nn`). Published research is not stakeholder-validated by being published.

## 1. What changed

The earlier Stage 6 draft treated "a governed, offline, community-to-programme workflow" as the candidate difference. The external research materially narrows that.

| Before the update                                                   | After the update                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Landscape unresearched; differentiation tested only in the abstract | Six Nigeria-relevant sources reviewed (some only partly retrievable); see section 3                          |
| Standalone application implicitly the working form                  | **Standalone is no longer the default assumption**; it must be justified against integration (H4)            |
| "Closed loop" described broadly                                     | Reframed as the **community-to-action layer**: what happens _after_ a signal, not signal generation          |
| Governance and WASH context presented as features                   | Governance is conditional on validated operation (H3); NTD + WASH is **not** sufficient differentiation (H2) |

**Positioning boundaries (locked for Stage 6 communications).** NTD-WATCH is **not** (a) another standalone NTD surveillance platform, (b) an NTD diagnostic or AI diagnostic product, or (c) a replacement for DHIS2, DHIS2 Community Health, WASHIMS, CIMS-NTDs or WHO tools. It is tested only as a possible interoperable community-to-action layer.

**Two primary validation questions (v2).** (1) Does a measurable operational gap exist between community-level NTD signals and coordinated public-health action in Nigeria that is not adequately addressed by existing surveillance, CHW, HMIS, NTD and WASH platforms? (2) If so, is NTD-WATCH best implemented as a standalone platform, an interoperable layer, or a capability embedded within an existing system? **Neither is answered.** The working hypothesis (an interoperable community-to-action decision-support layer) is a hypothesis, not a product decision; standalone carries the burden of proof. Outcomes allowed: PROCEED, MODIFY, INTEGRATE, STOP, INCONCLUSIVE.

**Three levels, never merged.** For every system: GLOBAL capability (what it can do) is distinguished from NIGERIAN implementation or configuration (what is set up) and ACTUAL FIELD PRACTICE (what workers do). Evidence read so far is mostly global capability; the latter two are unknown unless stated. The row-by-row view is in `ECOSYSTEM_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md`. AI stays out of the core v1 value proposition.

**Central question.** Can a lightweight, interoperable community-to-action workflow help close a real operational gap between potential NTD-related signals and timely, trackable public-health action?

## 2. How to read each hypothesis

Each hypothesis is written so it can be false. Fields: hypothesis; evidence held (labelled); evidence still needed; validation method; success condition; refutation condition; risk of being non-differentiated; outcome link. Numeric sufficiency is governed by the locked criteria (`STAGE6_DECISION_REGISTER.md` section 3), not set here. A user-interface feature never counts as differentiation.

### 3.7 What the comparisons do and do not show

They show that signal generation (CIMS-NTDs), frontline recognition (WHO app), NTD reporting (DHIS2 NTD), community tracking and supervision (CHIS) and NTD + WASH planning (WASHIMS) each have an existing Nigerian or WHO-supported counterpart, and that national direction favours integration. They show nothing about whether the segment between a signal and a tracked public-health action is adequately handled. **That segment is the only place a difference could lie, and its status is unknown (UK).**

## 4. Hypotheses

Numbering follows the Stage 6 update brief. The earlier draft's H1 to H7 are mapped in section 7.

### H1 Community-to-action loop (potentially the strongest hypothesis)

NTD-WATCH could connect community signals to structured assessment, referral, follow-up and programme action. Three components can be validated separately.

- **H1a Structured assessment and prioritisation (decision support, not diagnosis).** _Hypothesis:_ community workers lack a consistent, safe way to turn a potential signal into a structured assessment and a prioritised recommended action. _Evidence held:_ PF: prototype flow with demonstration rules; EF: WHO app provides clinical recognition (so the gap, if any, is prioritisation and recording, not recognition). _Needed:_ how signals are assessed today; by whom; what information used (XS, SS). _Method:_ WS1/WS3 interviews; WS6 expert consultation. _Success:_ stakeholders describe inconsistent or absent prioritisation that a structured prompt would help, and experts see a safe route; _refutation:_ prioritisation is adequately covered by existing training and tools, or experts judge a structured prompt unsafe. _Non-differentiation risk:_ high where a clinical tool or protocol already guides CHWs.
- **H1b Referral status and follow-up.** _Hypothesis:_ referrals are not reliably initiated, accepted, tracked and followed up, and information does not return from the facility to the CHW or programme. _Evidence held:_ PF: prototype stage lifecycle (facility side missing); EF: DHIS2 CHIS offers tracking, follow-up lists and supervisor dashboards, but referral support is unconfirmed (XE-06); UK: CIMS-NTDs post-signal workflow. _Needed:_ the real referral path, who owns completion, what happens when the person does not arrive, what returns from the facility (SS). _Method:_ CHW, supervisor, facility and programme interviews; paper mock of arrival/outcome. _Success:_ independent stakeholders describe unclosed referrals and no existing tool closing them, and facility staff state they would record outcomes within their workload; _refutation:_ closure already tracked, or facilities will not record. _Non-differentiation risk:_ high; referral and follow-up are common functions and CHIS may cover them.
- **H1c Aggregate programme intelligence and action.** _Hypothesis:_ LGA and state NTD staff would make a different or earlier decision with scope-limited, suppressed community-level signal, referral-status and follow-up information that does not duplicate DHIS2/NTD dashboards. _Evidence held:_ PF: synthetic views; EF: DHIS2 NTD analytics and WASHIMS exist; UK: whether any currently shows post-signal action status. _Needed:_ the decision list; lag; what is done on a signal (SS). _Method:_ programme and M&E interviews. _Success:_ concrete decisions named and no conflict with existing indicators; _refutation:_ no decision changes, or information duplicates existing dashboards. _Risk:_ high; dashboards are generic.

**v2 bar for H1c and H4:** because Nigeria already runs a government-owned DHIS2 NTD treatment-reporting platform at multi-state scale (XE-11, per brief), NTD-WATCH must show value beyond digitising NTD data or reporting; value lies, if anywhere, in post-signal action (assessment, referral status, follow-up).

**Outcome link for H1:** supported with no host platform able to run it → supports PROCEED or MODIFY; supported but hostable → INTEGRATE; refuted → MODIFY (narrow) or STOP.

### H2 Signal plus vulnerability context

_Hypothesis:_ combining potential-signal information with community vulnerability context helps programme staff prioritise action at community level. _Evidence held:_ EF: Nigeria already combines NTD and WASH indicators for four preventive-chemotherapy NTDs at national and state level on WASHIMS (XE-08); PF: the prototype index is a placeholder; UK: community-level use. _Needed:_ whether officers lack community-level context and would use it; data availability at that level; compatibility with the agreed thresholds (SS, GIS, WASH stakeholders). _Success:_ named community-level decisions that the existing planning does not support and a viable data source; _refutation:_ no such decisions, data absent, or the existing dashboards suffice. _Risk:_ **not inherently novel**; value must be shown at operational level. _Outcome link:_ refuted → remove from scope (MODIFY).

_v2 refinement:_ do not propose another WASH map. The testable question is: **can existing WASH information (WASHIMS, NTD-WASH indicators) improve prioritisation of NTD-related community action?** That remains a hypothesis (XE-08, XE-13, XE-14).

### H3 Governance-aware decision support

_Hypothesis:_ a validated rule-governance model and safe deployment process (approval, versioning, expiry, suspension, rollback, no-rule mode) produces meaningful programme value by making decision support safe to deploy. _Evidence held:_ PF: design only (`RULE_GOVERNANCE.md`); no-rule mode specified but **not implemented**; EF: WHO already provides clinical guidance content (so rule content should come from approved sources); UK: how clinical content is approved and updated in Nigerian CHW tools. _Needed:_ expert and programme views on whether such governance is needed and who would operate it; platform owners' view on content governance (XS, SS). _Success:_ experts and leaders state the need, will staff the process, and host platforms lack an equivalent; _refutation:_ existing approval processes suffice, or nobody will operate it. _Risk:_ **conditional**: a differentiator only if validated and operated; it is a process, not software. _Outcome link:_ supported → governance layer may be the contribution (often with INTEGRATE).

### H4 Standalone platform (to be tested, not assumed)

_Hypothesis under test:_ a standalone NTD-WATCH application is justified. _Status:_ **not currently supported as the default strategy.** _Evidence held:_ EF: national direction favours interoperability and fewer fragmented platforms (XE-09); EF: DHIS2 CHIS provides offline community capture, tracking and follow-up (XE-06); PF: prototype is standalone with mock server. _Needed:_ which platforms CHWs must already use; whether CHIS or another platform can host the workflow including referral, structured assessment and versioned content; owners' stance; reporting burden; cost, timeline, sustainability (SS, HIS, technical). _Options to compare:_ standalone; module or extension on an existing platform; interoperable service (reads/writes via standard exchange); other model. _Success (standalone):_ affirmative evidence that no host can support the workflow and that duplication burden is acceptable; _success (integration):_ a platform owner confirms hosting or material support. _Burden of proof:_ on standalone. _Outcome link:_ integration supported → INTEGRATE; standalone supported with H1 → PROCEED.

### H5 Primary user (supporting hypothesis)

_Hypothesis:_ the CHW (or trained community worker) is the right primary user. _Status:_ **assumption (AS), untested.** Alternatives: supervisor or coordinator, facility worker, programme officer. _Method:_ persona-specific interviews on capacity, time, devices, incentives, workload and willingness. _Success:_ the candidate named by independent stakeholders as both able and willing, with workload offset. _Outcome link:_ different primary user → MODIFY.

## 6. Standalone versus integration: decision logic

This logic is **locked** with the criteria in `STAGE6_DECISION_REGISTER.md` section 3 and is applied only after evidence is collected.

1. If the post-signal gap (H1) is not evidenced as real and important: **STOP**, or MODIFY to the narrow need found.
2. If the gap is real and a host platform (CHIS, DHIS2 NTD, another) is confirmed by its owner to host or materially support the workflow, and standalone duplication is not justified: **INTEGRATE**.
3. If the gap is real, no host can support it, the added burden is acceptable to CHWs and supervisors, a differentiator beyond NTD + WASH and community signal generation is evidenced, and the safety, privacy and ethics routes are feasible: **PROCEED**.
4. If the gap is real but the user, workflow, scope or positioning must change: **MODIFY**.
5. Conflicting or thin evidence leaves the outcome **OPEN** with a named next evidence step.
