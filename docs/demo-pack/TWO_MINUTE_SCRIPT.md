Status: for a real founder to narrate over the **actual running application**. Every screen named here exists in the current build (routes checked against `apps/web/src/routes.tsx`). All data is synthetic.

Spoken words below: **246** (target 230 to 260). At a steady 120 to 125 words a minute this runs about two minutes. Rehearse once with a timer; the on-screen actions in the right-hand column are the part most likely to overrun, so rehearse the click path until it needs no thought.

Before starting: follow `LIVE_DEMO_CHECKLIST.md` (start the app, press **Reset demo**, confirm the DEMO MODE banner).

## Words to keep out of the narration

Do not say: diagnoses, detects, prevalence, hotspot, validated, production-ready, official, endorsed, pilot results, outcomes, "open source", "released". If asked, answer from `CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md`.

## Optional spoken closing line (not part of the timed 250 words)

"Everything shown is synthetic and unvalidated. The next step is clinical and privacy review with the right experts."

## If you are running long

Cut, in this order: the declined-consent detour; the expanded explanation; the facility notice. Never cut the demonstration-data statement, the "not a diagnosis" statement or the data-confidence cue.
