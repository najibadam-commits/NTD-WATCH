Spoken words below: **260** (target 230 to 260). At a steady 120 to 125 words a minute this runs about two minutes. Rehearse once with a timer; the on-screen actions in the right-hand column are the part most likely to overrun, so rehearse the click path until it needs no thought.

Before starting: follow `LIVE_DEMO_CHECKLIST.md` (start the app, press **Reset demo**, confirm the DEMO MODE banner).

## Words to keep out of the narration

Do not say: diagnoses, detects, prevalence, hotspot, validated, production-ready, official, endorsed, pilot results, outcomes, "field tested", "partnered with the Board" (until a signed letter is held). You may say "open source": the code is public under the Apache License 2.0, as a prototype. If asked, answer from `CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md`.

## If asked about testing

Do not say the tests are stable, all green or bug-free, and do not say reset and sync problems are eliminated. Say: "The latest reported unit-test run passed in full, including regression tests for a demo reset and sync fix. An earlier intermittent test failure was not conclusively diagnosed. The fix is a demo-consistency mechanism, not a security control. There has been no clinical validation, field testing or security review."

## Optional spoken closing line (not part of the timed 250 words)

"Everything shown is synthetic and unvalidated. The next step is clinical and privacy review with the right experts."

## If you are running long

Cut, in this order: the declined-consent detour; the expanded explanation; the facility notice. Never cut the demonstration-data statement, the "not a diagnosis" statement or the data-confidence cue.
